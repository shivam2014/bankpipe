BankPipe

A portfolio tracker that runs in your browser, not on someone else's server.

BankPipe reads your bank statements, bonds and fixed deposits, and builds one ledger you can actually check. It stores everything on your own device. Connecting Google Drive is optional.

Where your data lives
  • On your device by default. No account required.
  • In your Google Drive only if you connect it — and only in a folder the app creates.
  • Encrypted before upload. Backups are AES-GCM encrypted; your passphrase never leaves your device.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no third-party scripts.

What it does

  • Import bank and brokerage statements without sending them anywhere.
  • Track bonds, fixed deposits and cash flow in one ledger.
  • Keep working offline; sync when you choose.

What it does not do

  • It does not ask for your bank password.
  • It does not upload your statements to us.
  • It does not sell, share or analyse your data.
