Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026 · BankPipe

BankPipe is a local-first application. This policy describes every way data can move, including the optional Google Drive connection.

1. Data we collect

None on our servers. BankPipe has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking pixels. We do not operate a database of user data.

2. Data stored on your device

Your portfolio, imported statements and settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. This data is not transmitted anywhere unless you explicitly enable a sync destination.

3. Optional Google Drive connection

If you choose to connect Google Drive, BankPipe requests one permission:

  • Drive file access limited to files this app creates (drive.file). BankPipe cannot see, read or modify any other file in your Drive.

When connected, BankPipe writes encrypted backup snapshots into a BankPipe folder in your own Drive. The data remains yours; we have no access to it.

4. Encryption

Backups are encrypted on your device with AES-GCM before upload. The encryption passphrase never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or to Google. Google stores ciphertext it cannot read.

5. Sign-in handling

Google sign-in is brokered by a small server component whose only job is to exchange an authorization code for tokens. It does not receive, proxy or store your portfolio data, statements or encryption keys.

6. What we never do

  • We do not ask for or store your bank credentials or passwords.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data.
  • We do not use your data to train models or for advertising.
  • We do not transfer your data to third parties.

7. Deleting your data

  • Local data: clear the site's storage in your browser, or use the in-app delete option.
  • Drive backups: delete the BankPipe folder in your Google Drive.
  • Access: revoke the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deleting the app's access does not delete files already in your Drive; you control those.

8. Data we receive from Google

BankPipe's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the backup and restore features visible in the app.

9. Children

BankPipe is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app.

11. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue.
